about

Every round is a real frame from a dashcam, filmed on a year-long drive around the United States in 2018. Five rounds a day, the same five for everyone. Practice rounds are unlimited.

The footage comes from A Dana Life, which streams that drive around the clock — the whole trip, in order, forever. On Twitch a chat bot runs the same game against the live feed: type !guess to call where the van is right now.

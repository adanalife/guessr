You can now send someone a link to a game you finished, and they'll see the map you saw at the end — where each of your pins landed, and how far each one was from the real place. The link lives in the about panel, under "your games", alongside every daily you've played. A game only becomes shareable once its day has finished playing everywhere, so a link can never spoil a round somebody hasn't had their turn at yet. Opening one greets you by naming whose game you're looking at and which day it was, and the board underneath asks whether you can beat it.

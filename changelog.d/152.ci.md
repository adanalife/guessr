**`test_clip_name.py` pins the one naming rule three programs implement separately.** `make_rounds.clip_name` writes `<slug>-<milliseconds>.mp4`, the clips worker decides from that name whether the footage may be cached for a year as `immutable`, and `rebuild.parse_image` reads it back to find the moment to re-cut — none of them calling the others. The test reads the worker's regex out of its own source rather than copying it, so it cannot become a fourth thing that drifts.

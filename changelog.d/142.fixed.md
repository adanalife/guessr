Nothing in the game waits forever any more. If your connection quietly goes away mid-request — leaving wifi, waking a sleeping phone — the game now gives up after a few seconds and says so, instead of sitting there. The one you would have noticed is the guess button, which could stick on "Scoring…" until you reloaded the page; it now offers the guess again with your pin still where you put it. Loading a day, linking a second device by QR code, and the live tile on the end screen behaved the same way and no longer do.
